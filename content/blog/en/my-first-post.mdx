# Building a Healthy Plate<br /><br /><br />

Welcome to **DietlessAI’s first blog post** 🎉<br /><br />

Here’s a simple formula to follow:

- 🥩 **Protein** → chicken, fish, tofu, beans
- 🍚 **Carbs** → rice, potatoes, oats
- 🥗 **Veggies** → half your plate
- 🥑 **Fats** → avocado, olive oil, nuts

> Pro tip: Balance is everything.
